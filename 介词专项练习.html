📘 介词速查表（先看这里）

① 地点方位

介词意思例子
in在…里面in the box / in the bag / in Shanghai
on在…上面（接触）on the desk / on the wall / on the farm
under在…下面under the chair
behind在…后面behind the door
in front of在…（外部的）前面in front of the house
next to / beside在…旁边next to the bank
near在…附近near my home
between在两者之间between A and B
above / over在…上方（不接触）over the bridge

② 时间 in / on / at —— 「大 in 小 at，日子用 on」

介词用在例子
in年、月、季节、上午/下午/晚上in 2026, in May, in spring, in the morning
on星期、日期、节日当天、具体某天的早上on Monday, on 1st May, on Children's Day, on Monday morning
at钟点、中午、夜里、周末at six o'clock, at noon, at night, at the weekend

③ 交通方式

by + 交通工具（中间什么都不加）：by bus / by bike / by car / by plane / by underground
on foot = 走路（唯一一个用 on 的！不能说 by foot）
具体某一辆车 → in a car / on a bus（小车用 in，大车用 on）

④ 必背固定搭配

look at 看listen to 听wait for 等
good at 擅长interested in 对…感兴趣afraid of 害怕
late for 迟到full of 装满look after 照顾
talk to 和…说thanks for 感谢come from 来自
play with 玩a photo of …的照片look like 看起来像
五大易错点
① by foot → on foot；
② in Monday → on Monday；on the morning → in the morning；但 on Monday morning（有具体日子就用 on）；
③ at night 是对的，in night 是错的；
④ between 后面是两个东西，中间用 and；
⑤ 介词后面跟动词要加 -ing：good at swimming，interested in drawing。
